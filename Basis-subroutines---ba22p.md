### Collisions of an atom in a <sup>2</sup>P state and an atom in a <sup>2</sup>S electronic state


> BASISTYPE = 10
>
> System subroutine:  sy22p
>
> Basis subroutine:  hiba10_22p.F90
>
> Ref. M. H. Alexander, B. Pouilly, and T. Duhoo, J. Chem. Phys. **99**, 1752 (1993).

⚠️ There are no integer system dependent parameters for this case.

#### The definition of the real system dependent parameters is as follows:

- `ASO`: spin-orbit constant of <sup>2</sup>P atom
